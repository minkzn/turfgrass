# M11 Exam 2 Practice

127 questions modeled on the real Canvas quiz style, sourced from the M11 course PDFs (creeping red fescue, the red- and sheep-fescue species complexes, hard fescue, and tall fescue — identification, adaptation, culture, and uses), plus a substantial slice of textbook enrichment (Ch. 3, pp. 61-64: Pooideae/Poeae taxonomy, red-fescue and sheep-fescue complex descriptions, chromosome counts, cultivar lists, and endophyte notes for each species, plus meadow fescue and the transition-zone climate concept). Chromosome-count and adaptation comparisons across the fescue species are tested from multiple angles, including one place where the module page's own table (sheep fescue: 56 chromosomes) and the textbook (sheep fescue: 28 chromosomes) disagree — both are tested against their own source. 10 at a time.
